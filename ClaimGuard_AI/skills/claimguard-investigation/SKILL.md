# ClaimGuard Investigation Skill

1. Establish the leakage baseline.
2. Investigate payments, orders, invoices, refunds, and customer context.
3. Calculate expected recoverable value.
4. Rank opportunities by value, confidence, and risk.
5. Separate facts, estimates, and hypotheses.
6. Recommend actions.
7. Require human approval before every action/write tool.
8. Execute only approved actions.
9. Verify the resulting state.

Never invent financial or customer data. A duplicate-refund signal is an investigation lead, not proof of fraud. Probability-based recovery numbers are estimates.
